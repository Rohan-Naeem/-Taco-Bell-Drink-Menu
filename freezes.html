FROZEN FAVORITES

Taco Bell Freeze Menu

A Taco Bell Freeze is a frozen, slushie-style drink. Freezes come in two sizes only — Regular and Large — and most flavours cost Regular $3.89 and Large $4.39. The flavours below are the ones listed on Taco Bell's official online menu, checked on 7 November 2026. Prices and availability can vary by location.

Freeze flavors, prices and calories

  • MTN DEW Baja Blast Freeze — Regular $3.89 (150 Cal), Large $4.39 (190 Cal). The frozen version of Baja Blast.
  • Wild Strawberry Freeze — Regular $3.89 (150 Cal), Large $4.39 (190 Cal).
  • Pineapple Freeze — Regular $3.89 (160 Cal), Large $4.39 (190 Cal). Listed as limited time on the official menu.
  • Pineapple Dragonfruit Dream Refresca Freeze — Regular $3.99 (210 Cal), Large $4.49 (250 Cal).
  • Baja Blast dirty soda Freeze — Regular $4.09 (190 Cal), Large $4.29 (230 Cal).

How much is a Freeze at Taco Bell?

Most Freezes cost Regular $3.89 and Large $4.39. The two exceptions are the Pineapple Dragonfruit Dream Refresca Freeze (Regular $3.99 / Large $4.49) and the Baja Blast dirty soda Freeze (Regular $4.09 / Large $4.29). Taco Bell's Happier Hour offers a $1 medium fountain drink or regular Freeze from 2–5 p.m. at participating locations — check your local restaurant, because offers vary.

What sizes do Freezes come in?

Regular and Large only. There is no Small Freeze on Taco Bell's official menu. Fountain drinks come in Small, Medium and Large, so the sizes are different — see the full drink prices by size.

Blue Raspberry Freeze

A Blue Raspberry Freeze was not listed on Taco Bell's official drinks page when it was last checked on 7 November 2026. The flavours in the list above are the ones Taco Bell lists right now.

Freeze questions, answered

How much is a Freeze at Taco Bell? Regular $3.89 and Large $4.39 for most flavours.

What Freeze flavors does Taco Bell have? Baja Blast, Wild Strawberry, Pineapple (limited time), Pineapple Dragonfruit Dream Refresca, and Baja Blast dirty soda Freeze.

How many calories are in a Taco Bell Freeze? 150–160 in Regular and 190 in Large for the three main flavours; the Dream Refresca Freeze is 210 / 250.

Is the Pineapple Freeze limited time? Yes, it is listed as limited time on the official menu.

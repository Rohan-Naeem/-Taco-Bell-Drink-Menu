TACO BELL FAVORITE

Mountain Dew® Baja Blast®

What is Baja Blast? It is Taco Bell's teal MTN DEW drink, one of the best-known Taco Bell specialty drinks. What flavor is Baja Blast? A tropical lime flavour. What is Baja Blast made of? At its base it is MTN DEW, served at Taco Bell as a fountain drink or as a Taco Bell Baja Blast Freeze. How much is a Baja Blast at Taco Bell? As a fountain drink: Small $2.69 (220 Cal), Medium $2.99 (260 Cal), Large $3.19 (420 Cal). As a Taco Bell Baja Blast Freeze: Regular $3.89 (150 Cal), Large $4.39 (190 Cal). Does Baja Blast have caffeine? Yes, caffeine is listed in its ingredients, but Taco Bell does not publish a caffeine amount in mg. It is available in participating locations and may also appear in different forms or limited-time variations.

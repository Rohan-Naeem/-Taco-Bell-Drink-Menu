ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

About Taco Bell Drink Menu USA

Taco Bell Drink Menu USA is an independent informational website that helps people explore Taco Bell drinks in the United States: fountain drinks, Freezes, refreshers, coffee, prices by size and calories. It is not a Taco Bell website, and it is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Taco Bell Corp.

What this site covers

The Taco Bell drinks menu on this site covers the drinks listed on Taco Bell's official online menu, including MTN DEW Baja Blast, Baja Blast Freeze, Wild Strawberry Freeze, Agua Refresca, Energy drinks and Cinnabon Delights coffee. Prices are shown by size where Taco Bell lists them that way: Small, Medium and Large for fountain drinks, and Regular and Large for Freezes. Calories are shown for the sizes listed.

Where the information comes from

Prices and calories on this site come from Taco Bell's official online menu (tacobell.com), checked on 7 November 2026. Taco Bell does not publish one national price, so prices can vary by restaurant, and menu items and availability can change. Always check your selected Taco Bell location for the current price and menu before ordering. The drink photos on this site are illustrative images created for this site; they are not official Taco Bell product photos.

Why this site exists

Taco Bell's own menu shows one price at a time and keeps prices behind its ordering flow, and most other menu sites list one flat price per drink with no size split. This site exists to answer the questions people actually search for: how much a drink costs by size, how many calories it has, what Baja Blast is made of, and whether a drink is still listed. See the frequently asked questions on the home page for the short answers.

If you spot a price or a drink that has changed, please contact us and we will check it against the official menu. Read the Privacy Policy and the Disclaimer for how this site handles information and its limits.

SPECIALTY DRINKS

Taco Bell Specialty Drinks

Taco Bell specialty drinks are everything beyond standard fountain soda: the Baja Blast family, Freezes, dirty sodas, Agua Refresca, Dream Refresca, energy drinks and Cinnabon Delights coffee. The list below covers every specialty drink on Taco Bell's official online menu, checked on 7 November 2026, with prices and calories. Prices and availability can vary by location.

Baja Blast and its varieties

Baja Blast is the most popular Taco Bell specialty drink — a tropical lime MTN DEW drink sold at Taco Bell since 2004. As a fountain drink it costs Small $2.69 (220 Cal), Medium $2.99 (260 Cal) and Large $3.19 (420 Cal). The family also includes Baja Blast Zero Sugar (Large, 15 Cal), Baja Midnight (same prices; 220 / 280 / 420 Cal), the Baja Blast Freeze (Regular $3.89 / Large $4.39) and the Baja Blast dirty soda ($2.99–$3.49 by size).

Freezes

Freezes are Taco Bell's frozen, slushie-style drinks, in Regular and Large only. Most cost Regular $3.89 and Large $4.39: Baja Blast Freeze, Wild Strawberry Freeze and the limited-time Pineapple Freeze. The Pineapple Dragonfruit Dream Refresca Freeze costs $3.99 / $4.49, and the Baja Blast dirty soda Freeze costs $4.09 / $4.29. See the full Taco Bell Freeze menu.

Dirty sodas

A dirty soda is a fountain drink with sweet cream and flavour added. The Baja Blast dirty soda costs Small $2.99 (270 Cal), Medium $3.29 (320 Cal) and Large $3.49 (460 Cal). Other dirty sodas cost Large $3.49, including Baja Blast Zero Sugar (60 Cal), Baja Midnight (470 Cal), PEPSI (430 Cal) and Tropicana dirty lemonade (170 Cal). You can also "make it dirty" on any fountain drink for an extra $0.30.

Agua Refresca and Dream Refresca

Agua Refresca drinks are cold, fruity and non-frozen, served in Medium only at $3.89: Strawberry Passionfruit (170 Cal), Mango Peach (170 Cal) and Dragonfruit Berry (180 Cal). Dream Refresca drinks are creamier and cost $4.19 in Medium only: Strawberry Dream (320 Cal) and Pineapple Dragonfruit Dream (330 Cal), both listed as limited time.

Energy drinks

Taco Bell lists two energy drinks: Pineapple Citrus Energy and Strawberry Tropics Energy, both Medium only, $4.49 and 50 calories. Taco Bell does not list Powerade; its sports drink is G2 Gatorade Fruit Punch (Large $3.19, 80 Cal).

Coffee and other drinks

Coffee is served in Regular size only: Premium Hot Coffee $2.39 (0 Cal), Hot Cinnabon Delights Coffee $2.89 (160 Cal), Regular Iced Coffee $2.19 (0 Cal) and Iced Cinnabon Delights Coffee $2.89 (160 Cal). Tropicana Orange Juice costs $2.29 (140 Cal, Regular). The Drinks Party Pack costs $4.39.

Specialty drink prices at a glance

  • Baja Blast fountain: $2.69 / $2.99 / $3.19 by size.
  • Freezes: Regular $3.89 / Large $4.39 (most flavours).
  • Dirty sodas: $2.99–$3.49 by size; make any fountain drink dirty for +$0.30.
  • Agua Refresca: $3.89, Medium only.
  • Dream Refresca: $4.19, Medium only.
  • Energy drinks: $4.49, Medium only.
  • Coffee: $2.19–$2.89, Regular only.

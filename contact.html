Taco Bell Drink Menu USA is an independent informational website run by a small team, not by Taco Bell. We welcome corrections and suggestions about this site — a price that has changed, a drink that is no longer listed, a new drink we have missed, or an improvement to a page. Every report is checked against Taco Bell's official online menu before anything is changed.

What to write about

  • A price or calorie figure on this site that no longer matches Taco Bell's official online menu.
  • A drink that has been added to, or removed from, the Taco Bell drinks menu.
  • A mistake on any page, or a question this site does not answer yet.

When you report a price, it helps to say which drink, which size and which restaurant area you saw it in, because Taco Bell prices vary by location.

Orders and restaurant issues

This site does not take orders, handle deliveries, or deal with restaurant complaints. For ordering, refunds, or a problem at a restaurant, please contact Taco Bell directly through its official website at tacobell.com or through the Taco Bell app.
